# Ambiguity ledger

Every ambiguity found during Challenge or mid-flight implementation.
Status: `open`, `asked`, `resolved → DEC-NNN`, or `delegated → DEC-NNN`.

## Format

```markdown
### AMB-NNN — <short name>
- Domain: <taxonomy domain>
- Severity: blocking | material | defaultable
- Status: open
- Question: <what is undecided>
- Evidence: <what the repo suggests, if anything>
- Asked: round N
```
